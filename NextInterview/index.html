Frequently Asked Questions

How many interviews can be booked per day?

We accept only a limited number of interview bookings daily so each candidate receives proper attention and detailed interaction.

What interview types are available?

You can choose from software development, AI/ML, HR, communication, system design, DSA, cloud, cybersecurity, embedded systems, and many more interview domains.

Who will conduct the interview?

Your interview will be conducted by real human interviewers with experience in technical, academic, or industry-focused interview environments.

Is coding practice free?

Yes. The coding practice section is free. Students can try placement-style coding questions and check their answers instantly.

Are these official company questions?

No. The questions are company-style practice questions inspired by common placement patterns. They are meant for learning and preparation.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Sessions and practice questions are available for beginners, intermediate learners, and advanced candidates preparing for placements, internships, or professional roles.

Will I get feedback after the interview?

Yes. Every paid interview session includes personalized feedback to help improve confidence, communication, technical understanding, and interview performance.

Are AI/ML interviews available?

Yes. AI/ML-focused interviews covering Python, machine learning, deep learning, data science, and related topics are available.

Can I request a specific interview area?

Yes. You can select your preferred interview domain while booking, and sessions are arranged accordingly.

Will the interview feel like a real placement interview?

Yes. The sessions are designed to simulate realistic interview environments with live interaction, problem-solving, and communication assessment.

Can I book multiple sessions?

Yes. You may book multiple interview sessions based on slot availability.

How will I attend the interview?

Interview details and joining instructions will be shared through email after booking confirmation.

A few things to know.

Can I enrol in a course now?

The courses shown are planned learning paths. Enrolment is not open yet. Confirmed schedules, fees, and joining details will be added before registration opens.

How are courses different from live sessions?

Courses are intended to cover a subject through a structured learning path. Live sessions focus on one topic, with space for questions and discussion. Final formats will be published with each offering.

Can I still book a mock interview?

Yes. Use “Book an interview” to visit the existing booking page and view the available options.

Is coding practice free?

Yes. Use “Start practising” to open the free coding practice page. Company-style questions are learning exercises, not official company questions.
